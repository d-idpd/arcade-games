How To Play

Keep the ball in play and score when it passes the other paddle. The first player to 7 wins.

Controls

  • Phone: drag on the left half for Player 1 and right half for Player 2.
  • Keyboard: W/S for Player 1, Arrow Up/Down for Player 2.
  • Gamepads: left stick, right stick, or D-pad up/down.

Kid-Friendly Rescues

  • Stretch Paddle: a player who is behind gets a taller paddle.
  • Bubble Save: if a behind player barely misses, the ball pops back once.
  • Gentle Serve: after a point, the ball serves slower toward the player who is behind.
  • Sparkle Nudge: when someone is far behind, the ball bends slightly toward their paddle.
